# Logo Creation Guide

## Create these PNG files:

### 1. icon.png (1024x1024)
- Green background (#059669)
- Large white 'S' (top)
- Large white 'M' (bottom)
- Simple, clean design

### 2. adaptive-icon.png (1024x1024)
- Same as icon.png
- Foreground: S&M letters
- Background: #059669

### 3. splash.png (1242x2436)
- Green background (#059669)
- Centered: Large 'SM' logo
- Clean, professional

## Quick Creation Tips:
- Use Canva, Figma, or any image editor
- Keep it simple - just letters on green background
- Export as PNG
- Save in mobile/assets/
